# Use Google Analytics with a blanket cookie-consent banner

Europe is one of three primary target audiences, which argues for a privacy-first tool like Plausible (no consent banner needed even for EU traffic). We chose GA instead because it's free, and paired it with a consent banner shown to all visitors (not just geo-detected EU traffic) so GA only fires post-accept everywhere — simpler and legally safer than geolocation-gated consent, at the cost of showing a banner to visitors who strictly didn't need one.
